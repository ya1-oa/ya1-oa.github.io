Research platform · Prototype

The Hall

The Hall is my research platform for persistent AI systems, including memory, tool use, evaluation, and embodied interaction.

Current prototype

ASI Console is a playable, deterministic browser simulation. You have five turns to balance model evaluation, oversight, and research capacity.

Research question

How can a system expose what it remembers, what it can change, and what evidence supports a decision before it takes action?

Next experiment

Give each memory entry provenance, record tool requests in an event log, and replay identical scenarios to measure which decisions change when evidence changes.
