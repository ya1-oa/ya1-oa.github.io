Start with a simulation you can replay

The first ASI Console experiment makes the consequences of each decision visible.

Each action changes a small state vector. Evaluation increases evidence. Oversight increases safety. Scaling adds research capacity and consumes safety margin. A fixed turn budget forces tradeoffs, and the decision log makes a run easy to inspect.

Why make it deterministic?

Two identical action sequences should yield the same result. That makes it possible to test individual rules, compare policies, and spot regressions when the rules change.

The next experiment

Add provenance to observations and compare runs with identical starting states but different evidence. Evaluate whether the resulting decisions change for the expected reasons.
