An absent offer is not a decline

Courier Radar estimates where a delivery courier might spend their next idle interval. The app can observe a location or an on-screen offer, but an observation gap does not tell it what the courier decided.

What the tests check

The dispatch suite distinguishes active delivery time from waiting time and excludes gaps in background GPS observations. Treating those gaps as observed waiting would inflate the model’s confidence in an area where it has less evidence.

The next review

A source-level smoke assertion expects an expired event in the capture handler. The reviewed handler does not contain the expected event literal. The next change should define the behavior at the event boundary and replay a synthetic screen sequence to test the data contract.
